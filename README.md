To verify that money collected is completely recorded, deposited intact, and protected from misuse.

## Main audit procedures
### Test weekly collections
#### • Selected sample of 6 Sundays and services, for each selected date:
##### 1. Obtained the official counting sheet
##### 2. Recalculated the cash and cheque totals
##### 3. Separated tithes, general offerings, and special offerings.
##### 4. Agreed the counter-sheet total to the cashbook and general ledger.
##### 5. Agreed the total to the bank deposit slip
##### 6. Traced the deposit to the bank statement.
##### 7. Investigated the differences and delays.


## Test counting controls
### Checked wether:
##### 1. At least two unrelated people count the money together.
##### 2. The counters sign the counting sheet.
##### 3. The pastor, treasurer or financial secretary does not control the entire process
##### 4. The money remains under the dual custody until deposited.
##### 5. Cash is deposited the same day or the next business day where practicable


## Test bank and mobile money depsoits
#### For each sampled collection:
#### 1. Match the counter sheet to the deposit slip
#### 2. Confirm the deposit date
#### 3. Trace the deposit to the bank statement
#### 4. Check whether the full amount was deposited
#### 5. Review Mpesa and other mobile money statements separattely
#### 6. Investigated deposits that are late, incomplete or not traceable.


## Test recording of tithes.
### Check if the church keeps records of individual's giving
##### 1. select a sample of tithes from counting records
##### 2. Agree them to the members' contribution record
##### 3. Check whether the amount was recorded under correct category.
##### 4. Confirm that adjustments or collections were authorized.

## Findings
### Finding 1 : Offerings deposits after several days
### Criteria : 
##### • Collections should be deposited promptly and should remain under appropriate dual custody until deposit.
### Condition :
##### • Two sampled collections were deposited three and four days after they were counted.
### Risk: 
##### • cash may be lost, stolen or used temporarily before being deposited.
### Recomendations:
##### • Require deposits on the same day or next business day. Where this is not possible, two authorized people should secure the funds in a restricted safe and document the reason for the delay.


### Finding 2 : Only one person counted the offering
### Criteria: 
#### • Counting collection should be done by at least two unrelated people
### Condition:
#### • Special offering was counted but one person while other was absent
### Risk : 
#### • Cash counting may be inaccurate since the other people is to recount confirm accuracy
### Recommendation : 
#### • Require notice of absence be given to the finance office to ensure another person is assigned.


### Suggested conclusion
#### • Based on the procedures performed, offerings and tithes were generally recorded and deposited, except for the exceptions noted in this report. The main weakness identified related to delayed deposits, incomplete counting documentation and inadequate segregation of duties. The church should strengthen dual counting, prompt banking, independent reconciliation and review of collections.
